Celsius to Fahrenheit

Type either side. The other side updates. The buttons are temperatures these DTF notes actually use.

These are starting points for ready-made DTF transfers. Always follow the instructions supplied with your transfers, then test on a scrap. The number on a heat press dial is often not the true platen temperature.

The formula is the ordinary one: Fahrenheit equals Celsius times 9/5, plus 32. This page uses that conversion beside every Celsius figure. 150°C is 302°F. 160°C is 320°F. 130°C is 266°F.

If a supplier prints both units and they do not match the formula exactly, trust the pair on their sheet. A press dial in Fahrenheit is not a reason to add an extra 30 degrees. If the dial and the results disagree, test a scrap before you invent an offset.

HTV and sublimation use other temperatures. Those converters and charts are on Heat press settings.
